About Me

Hey! I'm Shree Krishna Karki — a student and tech enthusiast proudly from Ramechhap, a scenic hilly district in Bagmati Province, Nepal. Known for its lush landscapes, the majestic Khimti river, and the vibrant Charikot bazaar, Ramechhap has shaped me into someone who thinks big and never stops climbing. 🏔️

My goal is bold and clear — to become a successful person in the tech industry. Whether that means building impactful software, launching a startup, or creating solutions that uplift communities like mine back home, I'm working toward it every single day.

When I'm not grinding code or dreaming about my next venture, you'll find me deep in an online game, watching anime late into the night, or planning my next travel adventure. Life's too short not to explore — both the digital and the physical world! 🎮✈️
